How it works

From two PDFs to a weighted plan.

1

Add your PDFs

Upload your syllabus and previous-year paper on the Sources page — drag, drop, done.
2

AI extracts structure

Gemini pulls topics with their units from the syllabus, and every question from the paper.
3

Questions meet topics

Each PYQ is mapped to its syllabus topic, producing real per-unit exam weightage.
4

Hours follow weightage

Enter your total hours — StudyFlow splits them proportionally and exports a PDF plan.
Features

Everything below runs on real data.

Unit weightage analysis

See which units actually dominate the exam, counted from mapped previous-year questions.

PYQ frequency intelligence

Every topic carries its real appearance count, so repeated questions rise to the top.

Weightage-based planner

Give it 10 hours or 60 — recommended time per unit is allocated by exam weight, live.

One-click PDF report

Export the full plan as a printable report generated on the server with ReportLab.

AI extraction

Topics, questions and mappings are extracted by Gemini, Groq or the offline ML engine — always validated JSON.
